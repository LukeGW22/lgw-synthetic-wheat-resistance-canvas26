This page looks closer at the breeding lines: where their resistance maps, which signals hold across models, what each locus does, and how loci stack. Breeding lines are the 2024–2026 TAMU advanced lines with the TAM 114 × synthetic backcross (SynBC) lines removed.

3Which signals hold across models

Each trait was fitted with six kinship-based GAPIT models (MLM, CMLM, MLMM, SUPER, FarmCPU, BLINK) on two phenotype scales (glmer % and empirical logit), 12 fits in all. Each square is one fit where the signal passed Bonferroni 0.05. A signal found in most fits does not depend on the model or the scale.
